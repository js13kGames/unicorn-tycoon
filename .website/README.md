You are the unicorn who builds parks.

And today, all you've got is a green field, one carousel and thirteen days to turn it into the happiest park in the world.

It's a **cozy** little park-building game: **tap** to raise rides, snack stands and benches, open the gates at noon, and keep the crowd fed, watered and entertained.

![Gameplay recording: the unicorn raises the last building on the isometric meadow, the park opens and the first visitors walk in](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Naeliah/Unicorn-Tycoon-js13k/refs/heads/main/media/gameplay-landscape.gif)

Rides wear out, so zap them back to life with a rainbow beam.

Happy visitors pay more, word gets around, and the arch over your gate lights up one rainbow at a time.

**Thirteen days**, then the Grand Parade.

Ready?

playable on [wavedash](https://wavedash.com/games/unicorn-tycoon)
